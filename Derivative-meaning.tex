\Section{Derivative---meaning of the concept \Star}

Given a function $f$ and a number $a$ in its domain, the original $f$ and its derivative $f'$ have the following meanings:

\begin{ProblemSet}[pencil space=1in]
 \begin{Problem}
  $f(a)$ is \dots
 \end{Problem}
 \begin{Problem}
  $\bigl(a, f(a) \bigr)$ is \dots
 \end{Problem}
 \begin{Problem}
  Physical meaning:
  $f'(a)$ is\dots
 \end{Problem}
 \begin{Problem}
  Geometric meaning:
  $f'(a)$ is\dots
 \end{Problem}
 \begin{Problem}
  Tangent line:
  $f'(a)$ is\dots
 \end{Problem}
\end{ProblemSet}
